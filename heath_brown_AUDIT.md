Verified, with a precise boundary. Once the exponent pair is fixed at the paper's choice `(p,q) = BABA^2BA^2B(0,1) = (11/53, 33/53)` (verified by exact A/B-process computation, and `4p+1-2q = 31/53 > 0` as Lemma 7 requires), every condition in the paper is rational-affine in the block coordinate nu and the slice parameter gamma. The system is certifiable end to end. Jointly in `(p, q, gamma)` the system is bilinear, not linear: conditions (26)-(27) contain the products `gamma*p` and `gamma*q`, and Lemma 7's term caps contain `nu*p` and `nu*q`. The exponent-pair choice is therefore a design substitution in the sense of the schema's sandwich discipline (item 2), and optimization over the exponent-pair hull lies outside the certifiable linear layer (step 4 scope boundary). The instance certifies the layer the paper actually publishes.

## Encoding summary

Variable `nu = log_N Y`, parameter `gamma = 1/c`. Design substitutions, all the paper's own: `u = N^{1-gamma}` and `v = N^{5gamma-4}` are Lemma 4's type II window taken at full strength, and `z = N^{e(gamma)}` with `e = (331-362 gamma)/31` is the minimum Lemma 9 admits, the same substitution the paper makes in its final condition `1-gamma+2e < 1` (pp. 262, 265). The certified threshold is invariant under relaxing z upward: any larger z raises the complement window's floor `1-2 zeta` further above u, so `2e <= gamma` remains the binding compatibility.

The piece `typeII_complement` transcribes the tension inside the proof of Lemma 3 (pp. 252-253): when no single factor lands in `[u, v/2]`, a pair lands in `(v/2, z)^2` and the complementary product ranges over exponent window `[1-2e, 9-10gamma]`, which the type II estimate must contain in `[1-gamma, 5gamma-4]`. Lower containment is exactly the paper's last condition (`gamma >= 662/755`); upper containment is exactly `v^3 >= 32x` (`gamma >= 13/15`). The Sigma_3' analysis under (11) generates the same two inequalities and is merged. The direct case (a single factor in `[u, v/2]`) carries no piece because u and v are the estimate's own window edges, covered by construction. The piece `typeI` has region `[e, 1]` with Lemma 8's auxiliary bound and Lemma 7's three-term exponent-pair bound as tools; term A's cap `nu >= e` is the constructed edge at z and carries no fail atom, since z is chosen precisely to make term A admissible on `[z, N]`. Reinstating that atom would plant a spurious facet witness at `nu = e` for all `gamma >= 569/662`, which is the worked example of why sandwich item 3 exists.

## Findings

**Finding 1 (facet map).** The binding facet at the published threshold is the identity's geometry, `u z^2 <= x` against the type II floor, not any single estimate. The witness at `gamma = 662/755` collapses to `nu = 1 - gamma = 93/755`. The runner-up facet is `v^3 >= 32x` at 13/15, dominated throughout. Ordering, exact: `662/755 > 13/15 > 737/874 > 5/6`.

**Finding 2 (Lemma 9's conditions are slack at the paper's design).** The typeI piece produces no witness at any certified slice. For `gamma <= 569/662` the region floor e already exceeds the Lemma 8 split `3/2 - gamma`, so the auxiliary bound alone covers `[z, N]`. For `gamma >= 569/662` the seam closes automatically because `569/662 > 737/874 > 5/6`. The printed conditions `gamma > (11+5p+3q)/(14+6p+2q) = 737/874` and `gamma > 5/6` of Lemma 9 are therefore strictly interior once z sits at `N^e`; the value 737/874 is the crossing of term C's cap `nu = (362 gamma - 256)/75` with the split line `nu = 3/2 - gamma`, and that crossing lies below the region floor throughout the certified range. The two K-side ablations make these tools load-bearing: removing either `K_aux` or `K_EP` yields witnesses at every slice (expected threshold 1), confirming both are necessary even though neither contributes a binding gamma-facet.

**Finding 3 (top-end artifact of the printed conditions).** At `(11/53, 33/53)`, condition (27), `e > 5gamma - 4`, is a genuine upper bound: it holds iff `gamma <= 455/517 = 0.88008...`. Beyond it, `z = N^e` violates Lemma 3's ordering `z > v`, and beyond `481/543 = 0.88582...` the paper's remark `e >= 1/3` fails, exiting Lemma 7's validity `Y >= N^{1/3}`. So conditions (26)-(27) as printed certify `662/755 < gamma <= 455/517`, and even the fallback choice `z ~ v` only reaches `gamma <= 8/9`. The theorem's full range up to `c > 1` rests on the standard flexibility of shrinking the `(u, v)` window (Lemma 4's proof permits any subwindow of `[N^{1-gamma}, N^{5gamma-4}]`) or on prior results, and I note that the prior published thresholds (Kolesnik `gamma > 9/10`, Graham-Leitmann `gamma > 62/69 = 0.8985...`) do not cover `(455/517, 62/69)` by themselves, so the window-shrinking argument is genuinely needed there. None of this affects the theorem; it is a presentational gap between the printed sufficient conditions and the claimed range, of the folklore-fixable kind. The ablation `no_typeII_estimate` isolates (27) exactly: with the complement piece's toolkit empty, witness existence reduces to region nonemptiness, certified threshold 455/517 with the facet witness `nu = 103/517` at equality and bracket slices at 22/25 below and 2201/2500 above.
